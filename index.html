The practice

Evara advises the people who run businesses and the people who invest in them. For founders, CEOs and executives, that means commercial strategy, operational support and getting ready to raise. For VCs, private equity and family offices, it means assessing opportunities and supporting the businesses they back. Having worked on both sides, Amar understands what each needs from the other.
